1. P1: `/api/health` in preview mode can return a false-positive healthy result after deployment drift. The handler at [scripts/shell-delivery-server.mjs](/home/pinto/repo/BMADGraphWebApp/scripts/shell-delivery-server.mjs:184) trusts `loadDeployedShellHealthPayload()` alone, and that helper only validates `dist/api/health.json` at [scripts/shell-bootstrap-metadata.mjs](/home/pinto/repo/BMADGraphWebApp/scripts/shell-bootstrap-metadata.mjs:136). If `release-manifest.json` or `support-matrix.json` is missing or schema-invalid while `health.json` remains from a prior build, health probes and startup waiters still see `ok`, but the shell immediately fails on bootstrap. That is exactly the delivery/fallback gap this story is supposed to catch early.

No other substantive scoped edge-case issues stood out after tracing the delivery server, Vite middleware, route fallback logic, and the added smoke coverage.
